About Me

I approach security like an adversary.

Overview

I’m a penetration tester specializing in external, internal and assumed breach assessments with a focus on Active Directory compromise, lateral movement and real world attack simulation. I lead engagements that go beyond vulnerability identification by demonstrating how weaknesses translate into business risk through full attack paths.

My work centers on answering one question: “If an attacker got in today, how far could they go?”

I bring experience across both offensive and defensive security roles which helps me focus on outcomes rather than isolated technical findings. My work includes:

  • External and internal penetration tests with validated exploitation paths
  • Assumed breach scenarios targeting domain environments and privilege escalation
  • OSINT driven reconnaissance and attack surface mapping
  • Reporting that communicates technical impact to both technical and non-technical stakeholders

Before moving fully into offensive security, I worked in IT and defensive security where I monitored large scale environments and conducted threat hunting. That background shaped how I test, validate impact and communicate risk.

Methodology

My methodology follows attacker behavior and aligns with structured frameworks such as PTES. Each engagement focuses on disciplined, scoped testing that produces actionable results:

01

Reconnaissance and OSINT collection

Intelligence gathering before a single packet touches the target.

02

Enumeration and attack surface mapping

Systematic identification of services, exposures and misconfigurations.

03

Exploitation, post-exploitation and lateral movement

Controlled compromise activities that reflect realistic attacker behavior.

04

Business impact validation

End-to-end attack chains mapped to risk, sensitive access and practical remediations.

I also contribute to knowledge sharing, methodology improvement and reporting standards so clients receive clear, defensible and useful outcomes from every engagement.
