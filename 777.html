Background

I'm a penetration tester specializing in external, internal and assumed breach assessments with a focus on Active Directory compromise, lateral movement and real-world attack simulations. I lead engagements that go beyond vulnerability identification — demonstrating how weaknesses translate into business risk through full attack chains.

// core question "If an attacker got in today, how far could they go?"

Before moving fully into offensive security, I worked in IT and as a blue teamer, monitoring large-scale environments and conducting threat hunting. That background shaped how I test today — I understand both sides of the wire.

Capabilities

I bring experience across both offensive and defensive security roles, with a focus on outcomes over optics. I've performed:

  • External and internal penetration tests with full exploitation paths
  • Assumed breach scenarios targeting domain environments and privilege escalation
  • OSINT-driven reconnaissance and attack surface mapping
  • Narrative-driven reporting that clearly communicates impact to technical and non-technical stakeholders
