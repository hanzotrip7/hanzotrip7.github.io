About

I’m a penetration tester specializing in external, internal and assumed breach assessments, focused on Active Directory compromise, lateral movement and real world attack simulations.

Before going fully offensive, I worked in IT and as a blue teamer. That background shapes how I test today. This blog is where I document the lessons, the wins, the defeats and everything in between.
